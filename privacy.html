PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 10, 2026

Your agents run on your Mac.
So does your data.

Agent Remote is a Mac app and an iPhone app made by Matthew Slotkin (“we”). The iPhone app is a remote control for coding agents that run on a Mac you own. This policy explains what each app stores, what passes through our relay, and what we can and cannot see. It is written to match how the software actually works.

The short version: we do not collect your conversations, files, prompts, voice recordings, or account credentials. There are no Agent Remote accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your data moves between your iPhone and your Mac end-to-end encrypted; our relay forwards ciphertext it cannot read and keeps no copies.

01 / WHAT THE APPS STORE

On your devices, for you.

On your iPhone
The list of Macs you have paired (name and relay route), a per-Mac access token and encryption key in the iOS Keychain, and your notification preference. Conversation history is fetched from your Mac when you open a conversation and cached only in memory.
On your Mac
Session history, attachments, the local knowledge base and memory notes derived from your conversations, device pairings, and the Mac’s own identity key, all under ~/Library/Application Support/AgentRemote/ with owner-only permissions. Nothing in that folder is uploaded to us.
Camera
Used only while you scan a pairing QR code. No images are stored or transmitted.
Microphone and speech recognition
Used only when you tap the microphone to dictate a prompt. Transcription uses Apple’s speech recognition, preferring on-device processing where your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple may process the audio under Apple’s privacy terms. The resulting text is sent to your Mac like any typed message.
Photos and files
Only the items you explicitly attach are sent, encrypted, to your Mac. Files your agent produces can be downloaded to your phone and shared through the iOS share sheet at your request.

02 / WHAT PASSES THROUGH THE RELAY

A courier that cannot open the envelope.

Your iPhone and Mac reach each other through a relay service we operate so that you do not need a VPN, port forwarding, or a fixed address. When you pair, the two devices agree on a shared secret using X25519 key agreement and HKDF. Every request, reply, and file is then encrypted on the sending device with AES-256-GCM and decrypted only on the receiving device.

The relay can see
Routing identifiers (random per-pairing values, not tied to your identity), the IP addresses of connecting devices, connection timing, and message sizes.
The relay cannot see
Message text, files, session names, project paths, voice transcripts, your provider credentials, or your encryption keys.
Retention
The relay has no database and stores no message contents. Undelivered ciphertext is held briefly in memory and discarded. Our hosting provider (Railway, United States) may retain standard connection logs for its own operational purposes; we do not use them to identify or profile you.

03 / MODEL PROVIDERS AND NOTIFICATIONS

Other parties, and when they are involved.

Anthropic and OpenAI
Agent Remote drives the Claude Code and Codex command-line tools already installed and signed in on your Mac. Your prompts, relevant files, and the excerpts used to build your local knowledge base are sent from your Mac to your provider account under that provider’s terms and privacy policy. The iPhone app never talks to a model provider directly, and we never receive those requests.
Apple Push Notifications
If you turn on reply notifications, your iPhone’s push token is sent, encrypted, to your Mac. Your Mac, using an Apple Push key you configure, asks Apple to deliver a generic alert (“Your agent has replied”) with opaque session identifiers. Notification payloads never contain conversation text or project names.
No one else
There are no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs in either app, and we do not sell or share data.

04 / YOUR CONTROL

Revoke, delete, forget.

Remove a phone
Revoke any paired device from the Mac; its token and key stop working immediately without affecting other devices.
Delete conversations and knowledge
Deleting a session on either device removes its saved history, attachments, and derived knowledge from your Mac. You can also forget individual learned notes. Deleting local data cannot recall information already sent to your model provider.
Stop everything
Quit Agent Remote on the Mac and remote access ends. Uninstalling the iPhone app removes its stored pairings and Keychain items.
Children
Agent Remote is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
