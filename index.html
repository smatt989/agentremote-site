UNDER THE HOOD

A remote for your Mac.
With the boundaries spelled out.

Your iPhone↔Encrypted relay↔Your Mac
Where does the work run?
On your Mac. Agent processes, project files, and saved history live there. Cloud models still process prompts through your existing provider account.
What can the relay see?
Routing information, IP addresses, timing, and packet sizes. Requests, replies, and files are end-to-end encrypted between your paired devices using AES-256-GCM.
What can my agents access?
Files and projects on your Mac, tools configured for the provider, and connected accounts exposed through those tools. Browser tools can use signed-in tabs after macOS and browser permissions are granted. Access depends on your setup and provider restrictions.
How many sessions can I run?
Create and manage multiple Claude and Codex sessions from your phone. Practical concurrency depends on your Mac’s resources and your provider’s limits.
How does the knowledge base learn?
Background model calls extract notes and synthesize linked pages from session evidence. Relevant context is retrieved for later prompts, and agents can search for more. Learning is incremental; recall and model interpretations can be incomplete or wrong.
Is the knowledge base local-only?
Storage and search are local to your Mac. Extraction and synthesis send excerpts to your existing model provider, and recalled knowledge becomes part of model context. The library is shared across your sessions, not isolated by project.
Which sessions work?
Codex and Claude sessions started in Agent Remote or through its terminal integration. An independently running client may need to be exited and resumed through Agent Remote first.
What access does my phone have?
A paired phone can direct agents with broad access to your Mac. App-created sessions request full access without ordinary agent approval prompts; integrated terminals retain their provider configuration. Pair only devices you trust.
What happens when I disconnect?
Backgrounding the phone leaves work running on the Mac. Sleeping the Mac makes it unavailable; quitting Agent Remote stops its hosted agents.
What do I need?
A Mac, an iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and a working Codex or Claude Code account. Provider usage and billing stay with your provider. This is an early release; arbitrary desktop and cloud agent clients aren’t supported.
